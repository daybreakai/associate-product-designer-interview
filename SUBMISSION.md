# Submission

Keep it tight — we're reading for judgment, not length. Bullets are fine. Delete these prompts
as you go.

## The prototype

Link, and anything we need to know to click through it.

## What I handed to Dawn

Which part of the planner's work you gave her, and why that part. If you deliberately kept
something with the human, say what and why.

## How I got there

The thinking behind the flow — what you considered and dropped, what you got wrong halfway
through, the constraint that ended up driving the design. We'd rather see the working than a
tidy story.

## How I built it

What you designed and prototyped in, and where AI did the work for you.

## What I invented

Patterns you had to make up because our product doesn't have them. Which ones you'd argue should
become components, and which were one-offs.

## Notes

Assumptions you made, what you'd do with more time, and anything you want us to know.
